Pixel Forge is a pocket-sized pixel art studio. Draw with pencil and fill, paint in mirror mode for perfect symmetry, and export crisp PNGs - all in your browser, nothing uploaded anywhere.

Open the studio
✎

Real tools

Pencil with smooth line interpolation, bucket fill, eraser, and a color picker that samples straight off your canvas.

🪚

Mirror mode

Every stroke paints its twin. Perfect for characters, icons, and anything symmetrical.

🎨

32-color palette

A curated palette plus a custom color picker, three grid sizes, and 40 steps of undo.

💾

Export PNG

One tap saves a crisp, upscaled PNG of your work - ready for avatars, sprites, and stickers.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
